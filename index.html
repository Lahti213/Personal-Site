01 — About

Accountant by trade, runner by habit

I grew up in Mobile, Alabama, and these days I live just outside Atlanta. During the week I'm an accountant, and most of my free time goes to trails, mountaintops, and a drone in the sky.

I'm happily married, we have a dog who keeps us moving, and we recently welcomed our first child. Since then I've dialed back the miles a bit and started lifting regularly. Turns out carrying a growing baby is its own endurance sport.
